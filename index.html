Appliance Cosmetic Grading Scale

We categorize all floor models into clear condition tiers so there are never surprises. Every single appliance is verified 100% mechanically functional.

Grade A: Open Box / Floor Model

Prong or carton damage during transit. The appliance itself has never been used in a residence and shows zero to near-undetectable cosmetic scuffs. Complete with original accessory packs and manuals.

Grade B: Minor Scratch & Dent

Superficial side, top, or rear cabinet scuffs that occur in distribution warehouse handling. Front faces and control touchscreens are clean. Perfect for fitted cabinetry where sides are hidden anyway!

Grade C / Factory Overstock

Excess production lots, discontinued seasonal finishes, or carton clearance units. Mechanically factory-fresh at steep bulk wholesale discounts.
